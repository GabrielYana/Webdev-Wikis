HOW WE GOT HERE

12010 – 2016

Rise

FaZe is founded in 2010 and enters Counter-Strike in 2016 by acquiring the G2 CS:GO team.

22017 – 2022

Win

Stars like NiKo, GuardiaN and olofmeister arrive. Sydney and Belo Horizonte 2018 follow, then Twistzz and ropz lead the 2022 Antwerp Major run.

32024 – 2026

Rebuild

GameSquare acquires FaZe in 2024. Two CS2 Major finals later, the roster is rebuilt in 2026 under head coach enkay J.
